# Journal of Global History — Submission Requirements
> **Publisher:** Cambridge University Press
> **Portal:** https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-global-history
> **Checked against the author instructions on 3 September 2026.**

---

## Why this journal

- Tvedt (2010), the argument the paper tests, was published here.
- Interdisciplinary readership: economic history, environmental history, global comparison.
- The Great Divergence framing (Netherlands vs Britain vs China) is the journal's home ground.

## Requirements and status

| Requirement | JGH rule | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Word count | 8,000–12,500 including footnotes | ✅ ~12000 incl. footnotes, tables and captions (PDF text count) |
| Abstract | 100–150 words | ✅ 150 words |
| Keywords | 5–10 | ✅ 7 |
| Referencing | Chicago footnotes, numbered consecutively, at foot of page | ✅ 72 real Word footnotes, Chicago short forms for repeats (word/footnotes.xml) generated by `build_submission.py` |
| Anonymity | Fully anonymised manuscript; author details on separate title page | ✅ repository URL redacted; no author names in body |
| Title page | Affiliation, email, postal address, ORCID, biography ≤100 words, acknowledgements, financial support, competing interests, AI-tool declaration | ✅ all present (`author_config.ini`) |
| Spacing | Double-spaced text | ✅ body double-spaced by `build_submission.py` (footnotes, tables single) |
| Figures | Digital, ≥300 dpi, individual files, placement indicated, captions with sources, alt-text | ✅ `data/fig1…fig5` at 300 dpi; '[Figure N about here]' + caption in text, figures appended; `figure_alt_text.md` for the accessibility form |
| Tables | At end of article with placement markers, heading and sources | ✅ seven tables in a Tables section, '[Table N about here]' in text |
| Data availability | Encouraged | ✅ statement + public repository + replication ZIP |
| Competing interests | Mandatory declaration | ✅ on title page |
| AI tools | Declare assistance in cover letter and acknowledgements | ✅ full declaration in both; methods paragraph points to it |
| House style | % with figures, year spans 1760–80, single curly quotes, British spelling | ✅ applied by `build_submission.py`; numbers under 100 in words left for copy-editing stage (flexible at submission) |
